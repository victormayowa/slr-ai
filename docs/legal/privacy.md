## What we collect

- **Account details:** name, email address, optional institutional email and ORCID iD, institution, position, and
  why you joined; your password (stored only as a bcrypt hash); whether you confirmed your email and accepted these
  terms; two-factor settings (the secret is encrypted and recovery codes are hashed).
- **Your Content:** records, documents you upload or retrieve, screening decisions, extracted data, appraisals,
  analyses, manuscripts, comments, and tasks, including the names of the people who made each decision (this is
  recorded in each project's audit trail, which is part of the scientific record of a review).
- **AI provider keys** you choose to save: encrypted, never shown again, and used only for your AI requests.
- **Billing:** your plan and subscription status. Card details are handled by our payment provider and never reach
  our servers.
- **Technical data:** request logs (IP address, time, route, and status) for security and troubleshooting, kept for
  [TO COMPLETE: log retention period], and error reports sent to our error monitoring provider without request bodies.

## Why we use it (and the legal basis)

- To provide the Service you signed up for (contract).

## How long we keep it

Account data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your account, after a 14-day grace period we remove
your profile details, saved keys, tokens, and notifications, and delete projects that only you belonged to with their
files. Your decisions and contributions in projects shared with other people stay part of those reviews, attributed
to "Deleted user", because removing them would break the review's audit trail. Backups are kept for
