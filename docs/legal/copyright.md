Full texts in OmniReview are stored privately in the project that retrieved or uploaded them, for that project's
review. They are never shared with other projects or customers, and are not published by the Service.

- **Open access** texts are retrieved from Europe PMC, PubMed Central, and Unpaywall, and each document records its
  licence where the source reports it.
- **Other texts** are uploaded by project members, who confirm they have the right to use them for their review (for
  example under an institutional subscription or a text and data mining exception).
- **Deposits** to repositories (Zenodo, OSF, Figshare) include only what a person chooses to deposit; do not deposit
  full texts you aren't licensed to share.
