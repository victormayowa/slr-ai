# Manuscript

## Starting the manuscript

Start the manuscript once certainty of evidence is signed off. Methods, results, other information, and the use of AI
are written from the review's records; the abstract, introduction, discussion, and conclusions start as PRISMA headings.

## Evidence markers

Section text is Markdown with three kinds of marker:

- `[#analysis:3]` links a sentence to recorded evidence (analyses, PRISMA counts, GRADE assessments, studies, and more;
  the Evidence panel lists them);
- `[@5]` cites reference 5, and `[@5; @6]` cites two;
- `[[table:sof]]` or `[[figure:prisma]]` on its own line embeds a table or figure built from the records.

## Verification

Every sentence needs evidence or a citation, and every number in it must match the linked evidence (rounding and
percentages are allowed). Sentences are marked verified, no evidence, numbers can't be checked, numbers differ, unknown
link, or cites a retracted work. Unsupported sentences can be acknowledged with a note; wrong numbers, unknown links,
and retracted citations must be fixed.

## AI drafts and language edits

The AI can draft a section from the evidence, or edit its language (academic tone, grammar, journal style, plain
language, conciseness). Suggestions show tracked changes and are applied only when accepted; edits that change markers
or numbers are flagged. Every save is kept as a revision you can compare or restore.

## References

References for included studies are added automatically. Add others by DOI or PMID, check them against Crossref and
PubMed (including retraction notices), export BibTeX, RIS, or CSL JSON, and import from or send to Zotero. Built-in
styles are Vancouver, AMA, APA 7, and Harvard; other CSL styles (for example `nature`) are applied on export.

## Checklists

PRISMA 2020, PRISMA 2020 for Abstracts, PRISMA-S, PRISMA-P, SWiM, network and diagnostic accuracy items, MOOSE, the AI
use disclosure, and an AMSTAR 2 self-check are filled in from the manuscript's headings and the review's records.
Override any item's status and location, and export checklists as Word or CSV.

## Authors and export

Record authors with affiliations, ORCID iDs, CRediT roles, and the corresponding author. Create a version for approval;
each author approves it (the corresponding author can record approval for co-authors without an account). Export Word,
PDF, LaTeX, or Markdown once every author has approved the current version.
