# Collaboration

## Roles

| Role | Can |
|---|---|
| Owner | Everything, including deleting the project and granting the owner role |
| Lead reviewer | Everything except deleting the project |
| Methodologist | Protocol, search, screening, adjudication, extraction, appraisal, analyses, GRADE, interpretation, manuscript, audit, workflow |
| Statistician | Extraction, running and approving analyses, manuscript, export |
| Clinical expert | Screening, extraction, appraisal, approving interpretation, manuscript |
| Screener | Screening |
| Extractor | Extraction and appraisal |
| Auditor | Read everything, audit trail, exports |
| Viewer | Read everything |

## Invitations

Owners and lead reviewers invite people by email with a role. The invitation link is emailed when the server has email
configured, and shown to the inviter to share otherwise. The person signs in (or registers) with the invited email
address to accept. Invitations expire after 14 days and can be revoked. An owner can transfer ownership to another
member; the previous owner becomes a lead reviewer.

## Competing interests

Each member records their competing interests and funding for the project. Declarations appear in the team report and
feed the manuscript's competing interests statement.

## Tasks

Create tasks with an assignee, due date, priority, and optionally a workflow stage. Assignees are notified, and reminded
the day before a task is due.

## Comments and mentions

Comment on a record, a full-text passage, an extraction cell, a manuscript sentence, a stage, or the project. Reply in
threads and resolve them when settled. Mention a member with `@` and their email address to notify them.

## Notifications

Notifications cover mentions, replies, task assignments and reminders, invitations, stage sign-offs, and living review
alerts. They appear in the app and, when the server has email configured and you haven't turned email off in Settings,
by email.

## Workload and reviewer metrics

**Team & Tasks** shows each stage's status and open tasks, what remains to screen and extract, and per reviewer:
decisions, throughput, agreement with other reviewers (Cohen's kappa), agreement with and overrides of AI suggestions,
extraction values, and appraisal answers. The team audit report (JSON or Word) summarizes the audit trail by person and
action, sign-offs, the audit chain check, AI use, declarations, and reviewer metrics.
